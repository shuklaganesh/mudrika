About the Event

Where Hyderabad Honours Its Own

The Hyderabad Startup Ecosystem Awards is a first-of-its-kind recognition platform dedicated to celebrating the extraordinary founders, companies, and enablers who have placed Hyderabad on India's — and the world's — startup map.

From deep-tech breakthroughs and fintech innovations to social enterprises and generational legacies, this awards ceremony acknowledges the full spectrum of entrepreneurial achievement rooted in the City of Pearls.

Held at the distinguished Samskruthi Hall, Raj Bhavan — the seat of the Governor of Telangana — the evening brings together 100–150 leaders from across the ecosystem for an intimate, prestigious ceremony befitting the magnitude of what Hyderabad has built.

Award Categories

Ten Ways to Change the World

The HSEA 2026 recognises excellence across ten categories that represent the breadth and depth of Hyderabad's startup ecosystem — from cutting-edge science to social impact, from early-stage promise to growth-stage mastery.

Category 01
Deep Tech & Innovation Excellence
Recognising startups pushing the boundaries of science, engineering and emerging technology to solve problems at scale.
Category 02
FinTech & Financial Inclusion Champion
Honouring companies reimagining how India manages, moves and accesses money — with equity at the core.
Category 03
HealthTech & Life Sciences Trailblazer
Celebrating innovations in healthcare delivery, diagnostics, biotech and life sciences improving outcomes for millions.
Category 04
Enterprise SaaS & B2B Excellence
Acknowledging software-first companies building products that power enterprises across India and the world.
Category 05
Consumer Tech & D2C Disruptor
For the founders who saw what the Indian consumer needed before anyone else did — and built it.
Category 06
Best Early Stage Startup
Spotlighting the most promising ventures under 3 years old that are already demonstrating exceptional potential.
Category 07
Growth Stage Startup of the Year
For the companies that have crossed proof-of-concept and are scaling with discipline, speed and purpose.
Category 08
Social Impact & Bharat-First Startup
Recognising ventures placing underserved communities and national self-reliance at the heart of their mission.
Category 09
Woman Founder of the Year
Honouring the extraordinary woman entrepreneur whose vision, resilience and leadership has set a new standard.
Category 10
Investor / Enabler of the Ecosystem
For the capital providers, incubators and mentors whose belief and backing has shaped Hyderabad's startup landscape.
Special Recognition · Lifetime Achievement
The Hyderabad Founder's
Legacy Award
Bestowed upon a founder whose lifetime of work has not only built a world-class institution but permanently elevated Hyderabad's standing as a global centre of enterprise. This award is not applied for — it is conferred.
